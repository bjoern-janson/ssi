# Localization Calculus

The V0.x ladder is best understood not as a sequence of benchmark wins, but as a causal localization program.

Each benchmark asked whether a previously adequate layer was sufficient for future correction. When it was not, the failure locus moved one layer deeper.

## 1. Common grammar

Adaptive systems repeatedly induce equivalence relations over possibilities.

An interface $O$ treats two states as equivalent when

$$
x\sim_O y
\iff
O(x)=O(y).
$$

A future task or transition property $T$ treats them as equivalent when

$$
x\sim_T y
\iff
T(x)=T(y).
$$

A Future-Sufficient interface requires

$$
\ker O\subseteq\ker T.
$$

If not, the interface is too coarse for the downstream corrective task.

The authority side is dual. If the evidential state $\mathcal E$ does not distinguish $x$ and $y$, a commitment map $A$ may not legitimately discriminate between them without additional licensed evidence:

$$
\ker\mathcal E\subseteq\ker A.
$$

The entire synthetic program can therefore be summarized by two pathologies:

1. **premature quotienting** — collapsing a distinction whose future consequences remain non-equivalent;
2. **unauthorized refinement** — creating a commitment distinction that the evidence has not earned.

## 2. Failure-localization rule

When adaptive correction fails, locate the earliest map whose induced equivalence relation is incompatible with either:

- future corrective consequences; or
- present evidential warrant.

Do not automatically blame the deepest mechanism.

A failed decision can arise because:

- the relevant distinction was never retained;
- the candidate set discarded the needed route;
- a present-valid commitment altered future transition topology;
- selection chose the wrong commitment;
- the selector's interface could not represent the needed distinction;
- relational semantics were not acquired;
- learned semantics were compressed before selection;
- evidence remained non-unique but commitment manufactured uniqueness.

These are different failure loci and license different revisions.

## 3. The ladder as localization

### V0.1 — missing discriminating probe

Preserved collision structure could generate a probe absent from the initial interface.

Licensed result: a mechanism unit test for generative failure.

### V0.2 — ambiguity

Generation survived noisy/ambiguous witnesses only partially.

The result separated interface correction from full interface discovery and exposed shortcut structure.

### V0.3 — novelty vs held-out generalization

Novelty $N$ and held-out collision generalization $H$ were separated, but usefulness $V$ still collapsed with $H$.

This forced a new distinction rather than allowing success on one metric to stand in for the others.

### V0.4 — stability is not future usefulness

The construction established

$$
N\land H\not\Rightarrow V.
$$

A stable, novel representation could still fail a fresh usefulness test.

### V0.5 — candidate contraction

Adaptive contraction improved future-route preservation relative to scrambling and aggressive fixed contraction, but did not establish superiority over conservative retention.

This negative result was important: Future Sufficiency could not be reduced to clever compression.

### V0.6a — hard budget is not a contraction objective

A max-budget policy remained perfectly Future-Sufficient while the preferred adaptive policy did not.

Hence

$$
\text{capacity feasibility}
\neq
\text{capacity opportunity cost}
\neq
\text{future sufficiency}.
$$

The preferred adaptive policy was honestly falsified.

### V0.6b — state vs transition

Presently adequate, equal-cost states could have different reachable future transitions.

This established

$$
\text{present sufficiency}
+
\text{resource feasibility}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{future transformability}.
$$

### V0.6c — selection as an FS locus

Among equally present-valid, equal-cost candidate commitments, structured reality-contact improved selection of states preserving future transition topology.

Thus

$$
FS_R=1\not\Rightarrow FS_{\mathrm{Select}}=1.
$$

### V0.6d — selection interface as an FS locus

Candidates were exactly equivalent under the frozen scalar interface while differing in future transition topology.

The construction and scientific run operationalized

$$
\ker O_{\rm scalar}\not\subseteq\ker T_{\rm transition}.
$$

A richer relational interface restored the missing distinction.

### V0.7 — relational semantics acquisition

The mapping from relational motif to future-transformability consequence was no longer hard-coded. It was learned from independently grounded cases, frozen, and transferred to unseen surface realizations.

This separated:

$$
\text{relational interface sufficiency}
\neq
\text{relational semantics acquisition}.
$$

Perfect raw-pattern training fit failed to transfer, while invariant semantic learning did.

### V0.7 junction diagnostic — semantics to selection

The 53 V0.7 treatment failures decomposed as:

$$
53
=
20_{\rm estimation}
+
12_{\rm evidence\ compression}
+
21_{\rm authority\ overreach}.
$$

This was diagnostic, not confirmatory, because the worlds had already been opened.

The decomposition nevertheless isolated the next causal junction:

$$
\widehat\Phi
\rightarrow
\mathcal E_\Phi
\rightarrow
\mathrm{Auth}
\rightarrow
\mathrm{Select}.
$$

### V0.8 — evidence to authority

V0.8 prospectively constructed fresh matched worlds containing both:

- **D3:** full evidence unique, point summary tied;
- **D2:** full evidence non-unique.

The primary endpoint was authority correctness rather than downstream score.

The preregistered test showed:

- point compression can destroy an authority-relevant distinction already present in the evidence;
- forced commitment can manufacture uniqueness from non-unique evidence;
- a correct downstream outcome can occur while authority remains incorrect.

This is the first preregistered fresh-world causal result directly separating CCA and CARS inside the synthetic ladder.

## 4. What the ladder did not establish

The controlled constructions do **not** establish:

- general safe self-improvement;
- a universal measure of corrective topology;
- general semantic learning;
- mechanistic identification of real adaptive systems;
- superiority of abstention under arbitrary real-world costs;
- that every form of information preservation is beneficial.

The ladder established a localization calculus under controlled conditions.

## 5. Program-level regularity

The recurring empirical object is:

> **Correction capacity depends on preserving the right equivalence structure through the adaptive pipeline.**

This gives the two-sided law:

> **Do not merge what future correction still distinguishes. Do not distinguish in commitment what present evidence still merges.**

And the architectural invariant:

> **No committed transformation may consume the evidential, representational, or authority pathway required to correct that transformation later.**

## 6. Why the V0.x ladder is closed

The ladder has already localized the major synthetic failure loci from contact through authority. Another increasingly elaborate toy world would risk optimizing the benchmark family rather than testing the theory.

The next scientific burden is external prediction:

> Does corrective topology measured now predict future adaptation in a genuinely independent domain after matching present capability and standard adaptability?

That is the transition from synthetic localization to empirical risk.
